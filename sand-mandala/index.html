sand mandala

A 30-second loop of simulated sand, seen from above. Invisible funnels lay down colour after colour, overlapping vortices stir it together, and a black void swallows the lot, closing as it feeds, until everything blasts back out and off the frame, leaving the bare plate to start again.

The sand is a height field with an angle of repose and a colour for every grain, so piles slump and avalanche. Only one quadrant is simulated and the render mirrors it, which keeps the symmetry exact.
