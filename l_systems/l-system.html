L-systems for language

L-systems usually grow plants. This one grows sentences. Each click rewrites the current string of parts of speech through a stochastic grammar — POS → (POS, POS, POS) — then picks a word at random from each bank below. Fill the banks with your own vocabulary. If it gets stuck, restart and try again.

Based on this project, and written up here.
